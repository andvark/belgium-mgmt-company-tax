# Hypotheses: how each was tested and what the model says

> **Every verdict is conditional on (1) tax rules supplied by the project owner and not confirmed against primary sources, (2) proposal parameters that are scenario assumptions, and (3) for the population-level hypotheses, assumptions about the owner mix, the size of S0 and the response of owners that no data identify.** 'Tested' means: run against the model under explicit sweeps; it does not mean the answer is known for Belgium. Generated by `scripts/make_hypotheses.py` from `outputs/`.

Summary: 18 hypotheses; 8 supported, the rest not supported, partly supported, conditional on an unknown input, or untestable with available data. Hypothesis H3 (prevalence of tax-motivated companies) cannot be tested at all without owner-level data.

## What would change the verdicts

- **Rules confirmed or corrected** (`hand_check_list.md`): H1, H2, H4-H8 move with the 18.82%/18% total, the lock counting, VVPRbis eligibility, the contribution base, and employee allowances.
- **The draft text** instead of second-hand excerpts: H4, H7, H8 and the budget hypotheses H13-H15.
- **Owner-level data** (director pay, retained profits, revenue, client concentration in S0): H3, H13-H15 and H18 become testable; the owner mix is the largest single driver of the budget result.
- **An estimate of how owners respond** (incorporation elasticity for Belgium): H9, H10 and H16.
